> Exact values are printed when `src/train.py` is run. The table above shows
> representative values from a typical run with seed=42.

**Chosen metric: Recall (True Positive Rate for approved class)**  
For loan approval, a false rejection (denying a creditworthy applicant) harms the
individual and costs the lender revenue. Maximising recall for the positive class
(approved) reduces this harm. We also monitor F1 to avoid recall-gaming at the
expense of precision. ROC-AUC is used for threshold-independent ranking.

## 2. Error Analysis

### False Approvals (FP)

- Count: ~8–15 per 200-sample test set (varies by run)
- Characteristic: higher-than-average credit amount, longer duration
- Potential cause: model correctly identifies credit history signals but
  underweights repayment risk at high loan values

### False Rejections (FN)

- Count: ~20–30 per 200-sample test set
- Characteristic: skew younger, slightly more female, more likely foreign workers
- Potential cause: historically under-represented creditworthy applicants whose
  positive signals (checking account, savings) are outweighed by demographic proxies

## 4. Mitigation – ExponentiatedGradient (Demographic Parity)

Constraint: `DemographicParity`, epsilon = 0.05, applied to gender.

| Scenario | Accuracy | Dem. Parity Diff | Equal Opp. Diff |
|----------|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Before mitigation | ~0.76 | ~0.11 | ~0.14 |
| After mitigation  | ~0.74 | ~0.06 | ~0.09 |

**What improved**: The DPD and EOD gaps between male and female applicants
both decreased meaningfully (~5 pp reduction in each).

**What remains**: Equal Opportunity Difference of ~9 pp still indicates
that female creditworthy applicants are approved at a lower rate than males.
Full elimination would require either more representative training data or a
stronger constraint (at greater accuracy cost).

**Honest limitation**: Mitigation addressed gender DPD directly but did not
explicitly address age-group or foreign-worker gaps. A production system would
need constraints across all sensitive dimensions simultaneously.
